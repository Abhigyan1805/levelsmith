Rules

Each move slides the ember in a straight line until a wall stops it. It collects every coal it passes, and each wet clay tile it leaves bakes into a wall.

Collect every coal, then come to rest on the exit ring. Sliding across the ring does not count.

Arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on the board.
